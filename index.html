How to use this plan

Keep days light: one highlight + one stroll. Door-to-door from Créteil often takes 45–90 minutes. Prefer buses when metro stations mean stairs. Check live access on Bonjour RATP or Île-de-France Mobilités.

Centre Pompidou is closed for renovation — skip Beaubourg. On 12–13 Sep, La Fête de Paris (free) is on; République may be crowded on the 12th — better avoid that area then.

Orsay · families

Friday 11 · Disneyland Paris

10:00 – 18:00
Fixed plan Under 3s free entry One park is enough

Soft day for a one-year-old: gentle rides, characters, parade, naps in the stroller — not coasters. Use the Baby Care Center; ask Cast Members for Rider / Baby Switch. Bring your own reclining stroller for naps (on-site rentals are basic and subject to availability).

Travel from Créteil: reach Saint-Maur – Créteil, then RER A to Marne-la-Vallée Chessy (destination must say Chessy, not Torcy). Leave around 8:00–8:30 to be at the gates by 10:00. Chessy station is largely step-free; a short flat walk to the entrances.

How to slot them in

  • Free slot morning of the 9th → Petit Palais Baby balade if you book.
