How to use this plan

Keep days light: one highlight + one stroll. Door-to-door from Créteil often takes 45–90 minutes. Prefer buses when metro stations mean stairs. Check live access on Bonjour RATP or Île-de-France Mobilités.

Centre Pompidou is closed for renovation — skip Beaubourg. On 12–13 Sep, La Fête de Paris (free) is on; République may be crowded on the 12th — better avoid that area then.

Day-by-day itinerary

Days are interchangeable with weather and naps. Prices and queues change — reconfirm before you go.

Tuesday 8 · East / easy start

Bois de Vincennes
Parc Floral Closest soft outing from Créteil

Half-day at the Parc Floral: playgrounds, collections, picnic vibe. Stroller-friendlier entry via Nymphéa when possible. Good first day after travel.

Wednesday 9 · Trocadéro / 7th

Views without the climb

Thursday 10 · Left Bank

Orsay · Berges · Tuileries
Short museum Flat riverside walk

Short Musée d’Orsay visit (lifts / ramps, stroller-friendly path), then the Seine banks and Passerelle Senghor into the Tuileries — classic couple atmosphere at baby pace.

Orsay · families

Friday 11 · Disneyland Paris

10:00 – 18:00
Fixed plan Under 3s free entry One park is enough

Soft day for a one-year-old: gentle rides, characters, parade, naps in the stroller — not coasters. Use the Baby Care Center; ask Cast Members for Rider / Baby Switch. Bring your own reclining stroller for naps (on-site rentals are basic and subject to availability).

Travel from Créteil: reach Saint-Maur – Créteil, then RER A to Marne-la-Vallée Chessy (destination must say Chessy, not Torcy). Leave around 8:00–8:30 to be at the gates by 10:00. Chessy station is largely step-free; a short flat walk to the entrances.

Disneyland Paris official RER A info

Saturday 12 · 6th arr.

Luxembourg + Fête de Paris
Jardin du Luxembourg Free festival

Slow morning in the Luxembourg Garden (main paths are stroller-OK). Optional La Fête de Paris at Place André Honnorat if crowds feel manageable. Avoid République this day if busy.

La Fête de Paris

Sunday 13 · Brunch day

Family table + short stroll
Reserve ahead Ask for high chair / stroller space

Family brunch then a short neighbourhood walk — or a second pass at La Fête de Paris if Saturday was too crowded. Reconfirm brunch prices the day before.

Monday 14 · 5th arr.

Jardin des Plantes
Garden free Ménagerie optional

Botanical calm. Garden paths are mostly fine with a stroller; skip the Palaeontology gallery (not step-free). Add the Ménagerie only if everyone still has energy.

Short Louvre visit (strollers OK; back carriers not allowed — use a front carrier or museum loan), then Palais-Royal / a calm tea stop. Or swap for rest / Bois de Vincennes if Disney wiped you out.

Wednesday 16 · Departure

Light morning only

Swap-in extras

Use these if you want to reshuffle a day.

Canal Saint-Martin

Flat-ish quays, terraces, couple vibe. Cobbles near the locks — take it slow.

Buttes-Chaumont

Beautiful but hilly — bonus only; stay on the lower paths with the stroller.

Montmartre (lower only)

Skip the summit stairs with a stroller. Enjoy the lower streets and ambience instead.

Packing tips

Compact stroller, front baby carrier, layers for September, light jacket for a Seine cruise, snacks & change kit.
